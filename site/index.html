Sequence search

Paste one protein sequence (FASTA or plain). The query is compared with every MicroRhodoDB record by a 3-mer prefilter followed by Smith-Waterman local alignment (BLOSUM62, gap open 11, gap extend 1), entirely in your browser. The ten best hits are listed with identity and coverage. This is a similarity search only: the full membership score (HMM domains, retinal lysine, scorer gates) requires the command-line tool tools/mrdb_search.py shipped with the release.

Novel-family candidate layer

Profile-HMM searches for published rhodopsin families that the membership scorer may miss (MerMAID, bestrhodopsin, schizorhodopsin, xenorhodopsin, viral rhodopsins) found no new candidates (funnel below). The candidate table is a structure-based watchlist: Foldseek hits (AlphaFold DB afdb50, MGnify ESM) of family representatives that are absent from the corpus and either carry a family-HMM flag for an under-represented family or lack PF01036 support. Candidates are kept in a separate table and never change membership or counts. Web-search hit lists were capped at 1000 per query and database, so the watchlist is not exhaustive.
